Event Rate by Period

Event rates should be broadly comparable across periods. A large gap signals population drift the model will have to survive.

Account Overlap Between Periods

This mart is panel data — one account contributes many monthly rows. Accounts appearing in more than one period are expected in a time-based split (an account observed for years spans all three), and are not the same thing as row-level leakage: no row's future outcome is ever visible in an earlier period. Reported here so the effect is explicit rather than hidden. A grouped split by trade_id is the alternative if strict account separation is required.
