Why {{ recommended.display_name }}, Not Just the Highest AUC

This dataset's own validation focus (see the data dictionary) is out-of-time AUC, Gini, KS, top-decile capture, and operational queue usefulness — not raw accuracy. A collections team acting on a fixed capacity cares most about how many bad accounts land in the top of the ranked list, which is exactly what top-decile capture and KS measure. {{ recommended.display_name }} leads on both: it captures {{ recommended.top_decile_capture }}% of all accounts that actually roll to 90+ DPD within just the riskiest 10% of the portfolio (KS = {{ recommended.ks }}).

If interpretability or regulatory explainability matters more than the last few points of lift, the Logistic Regression baseline remains a legitimate choice — its OOT AUC ({{ rows[0].auc }}) is close behind. This is a judgment call for the business, not a purely statistical one.
