Why these features are leakage-free

Every window is trailing: a rolling average covers the observation month and the two before it, and a change is measured against the previous month. Nothing reaches forward past the observation date, so these satisfy the data dictionary's rule — "use only information available on or before the observation date." No target column is touched during construction.

An account's first observation has no prior month, so its trend and volatility features are left as null rather than filled with zero — "unknown" and "no change" are different states, and the model should be able to tell them apart.

Signal Strength

Correlation of each feature with roll_to_90p_6m. A diagnostic to see which derivations carry signal — not a model, and not a substitute for the feature importances that come with training.

Note the pattern: rolling and stress features carry far more signal than single-month deltas. A one-month change in DPD is close to noise, while the 3-month average, the running maximum, and the count of delinquent months are among the strongest. Accumulated behavior predicts better than a single step.
