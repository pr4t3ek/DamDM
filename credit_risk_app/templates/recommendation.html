Key Business Recommendation

Use {{ model_name }} as an early-warning prioritization signal for proactive collections outreach — ranking active accounts by risk of rolling to serious delinquency within 6 months so limited collections capacity is spent on the accounts most likely to need it — not as an autonomous credit or account-action decision. The model's probabilities are ranking-optimized (via balanced class weights), not calibrated to true likelihood, and no demographic/fairness analysis was performed on this dataset; any customer-facing or credit-decisioning use would need both before deployment. Within that scope — a decision-support ranking that a human collections team acts on — {{ recommended.capture_rate }}% capture at {{ recommended.capacity_pct }}% capacity represents a substantial, actionable improvement over undirected outreach.
