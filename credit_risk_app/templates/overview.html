Predicting Serious Delinquency in Active Credit Accounts Using Behavioral Risk Analytics

Financial institutions need to identify active credit accounts likely to transition into 90+ Days Past Due (DPD) within the next six months. Early prediction of serious delinquency enables proactive intervention, reduces credit losses, and improves portfolio risk management.

Business Value

  • Flags at-risk active accounts before they become severely delinquent, ahead of standard bureau-cycle reporting.
  • Enables proactive collections outreach and credit-line management instead of reactive recovery after default.
  • Supports risk-based prioritization: focus limited collections capacity on the accounts most likely to roll to serious delinquency.
  • Feeds portfolio-level risk monitoring and early-warning dashboards for credit risk management teams.

High-Level Methodology

  1. Data foundation — behavior_risk_mart (this milestone): one row per trade-month, 31 columns, already joined with customer/geography attributes.
  2. Data quality & governance — validate the data and explicitly classify every variable as safe, leakage-risk, or target (see Data Quality and Leakage & Governance pages).
  3. Exploratory analysis & feature engineering — understand delinquency, payment, and utilization behavior; build rolling/trend features (planned, Milestone 2).
  4. Modeling — out-of-time train/validation/test split by month_end_date; Logistic Regression baseline vs. Random Forest / XGBoost challengers (planned, Milestone 3).
  5. Evaluation & deployment — OOT AUC, Gini, KS, top-decile capture; package as a scoring pipeline for an Early Warning System (planned, Milestones 3–5).

See ROADMAP.md in the repository for the full phase-by-phase build plan.
