Why keep these separate? The financial metrics are the source data's own generated benchmark figures (useful for portfolio-level comparison, not causal or predictive). The portfolio metrics are ground truth about what happened. The model metrics measure prediction quality against that ground truth. Blending them — e.g. treating pd_12m_proxy as if it were this model's output — would misrepresent both.
